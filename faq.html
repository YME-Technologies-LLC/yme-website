FAQ & Support

Questions, answered.

YME is a Miami dating app built around plans, not profiles. You post a plan, people who like it tap Interested, you pick who joins, and you meet in person.

The name comes from “why me?”. Say it out loud and it becomes YME.

Pick the activity, the day, the time, the neighbourhood and the venue, then post. It takes under a minute.

Coffee, padel, a rooftop: if you can plan it, it’s a date.

Your plan shows the activity, the time and the place. You choose who joins.

YME collects your location only while the app is open, and never in the background. The Privacy Policy has the details.

You see everyone who tapped Interested on your plan, and you pick one person. Then the chat opens, just to sort out the details.

One person, one date, one focus.

Meeting new people always carries some risk, and no app can remove it. Here is what YME does.

Your phone number is verified with a one-time code. With your consent, you also take a short live face scan. We compare it with your profile photos. This checks that you are a real person and that you match them. Verification is not a background check.

You can report and block other members in the app. If you are ever in danger right now, call 911 first.

More in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.

YME is free to use, with an optional Premium upgrade.

Live in Miami. YME is on the App Store, in the United States only. You must be 18 or older to use it.

Not in Miami yet? Get notified when YME comes to your city.

If someone doesn’t show up for a plan they accepted, you get your invitation back.

A first no-show can bring a warning. If it happens again, that person’s invitations can be suspended for a while. Repeated no-shows can lead to the account being suspended or closed. There are never any fines.

Cancelling because you don’t feel safe never counts against you, even at the door. The Terms of Use (section 9) have the details.

YME nights are coming to Miami. The idea: get a ticket and see who else is going and would like company. Then agree to meet one person at the door and walk in together.

Never walk in alone.

In the app, you can block and report other members. You can also report content, messages and profiles.

For anything else, email support@ymedate.com. If you are in danger right now, call 911 first. Reporting to YME does not replace emergency services.

You can delete your account at any time in the app. It closes immediately: you are signed out and your profile disappears.

Within 30 days, we delete your photos, messages, profile, location point and face verification data. Backups clear within 90 days. Some safety and abuse records are kept, as the Privacy Policy explains.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it separately in your phone’s subscription settings. For any request about your data, write to privacy@ymedate.com.

YME sends one kind of text: a one-time code to verify your phone number. There are no marketing texts.

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe, or HELP for help. If you opt out, phone sign-in is unavailable until you opt back in. See SMS Consent.

Still need help?

Email support@ymedate.com and we will get back to you.

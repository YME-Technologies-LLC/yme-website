The problem

Dating apps are good at matches. Not at meeting.

  • 78%

    of people who used a dating app in the past year feel burned out by it sometimes, often or always.

    Source: Forbes Health / OnePoll survey of 1,000 US adults, 2024 (opens in a new tab)

  • 79%

    of college and graduate students say they don’t use a dating app even once a month.

    Source: Axios / Generation Lab survey of students, 2023 (opens in a new tab)

  • +42%

    more people went to dating and singles events on Eventbrite in the US in 2023 than in 2022.

    Source: Eventbrite’s own platform data, dating report 2024 (PDF) (opens in a new tab)

And in three experiments, people said yes to fewer profiles the longer they swiped. Pronk & Denissen, 2020 (opens in a new tab)

A match is not a date.

Safety

Safety, by design.

  • 01

    Verified profiles

    Phone and face checks before you meet.

  • 02

    Public places only

    Every plan is an activity out in the city.

  • 03

    Face check, with consent

    A short live scan is compared with your photos. Delete your account and it is deleted within 30 days.

  • 04

    Block and report

    Block or report anyone in the app. In danger? Call 911 first.

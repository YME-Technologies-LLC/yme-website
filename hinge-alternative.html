Comparison

A Hinge alternative in Miami: YME vs Hinge.

Pick Hinge if you want a detailed profile and paid options. Pick YME if you live in Miami and want a date that starts as a plan: an activity, a time and a place that someone says yes to.

The difference in one line

On Hinge, a conversation starts when someone likes or comments on part of your profile. On YME it starts when someone taps Interested on your plan. Both apps want you to meet in person. They start from different places.

When Hinge is the better choice

  • You want a profile with room for prompts, photos, video and voice [2], and you like deciding from that before you meet.
  • You want paid options such as unlimited likes [5]. YME offers an optional Premium upgrade, but is newer.
  • You want an app from a company that is part of Match Group [6]. YME is newer.
  • You do not live in Miami. YME is live in Miami, in the United States only, so check where Hinge is offered.

When YME is the better choice

  • You live in Miami and would rather post “coffee in Wynwood, Friday, 6:30 PM” than write another opening message.
  • You want the host to choose one person for one date, with the when and where already settled.
  • You want every plan to be an activity out in the city.
